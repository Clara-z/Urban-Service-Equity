Urban Service Equity Dashboard
A Block-Level Equity Diagnostic for Resource Allocation in the City of San Francisco
City & County of San Francisco

Where are 311 services falling short — and why?

This dashboard maps every city block against an equity score that balances service performance (311 volume, resolution speed, service mix) with housing need (density, crowding, affordability, building age). Use it to identify underserved areas, understand structural root causes, and prioritize resource allocation across San Francisco's 41 neighborhoods.

How to use this tool

The map supports three analytical views. Start with Equity Score to locate underserved blocks, switch to LISA Quadrant to see spatial clustering patterns, then drill into Cluster to compare neighborhood archetypes and root causes.
